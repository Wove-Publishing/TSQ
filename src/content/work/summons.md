Should I ever tell you  
I come by it naturally,  
I’d be mistaken, of course.  
No one does this work  
Without searching the soul,  
Clinging to the body’s remains.  
Call it spirit, if you will.  
Those of us compelled  
To dwell in the shadows,  
Know no other life,  
Asking only for forgiveness,  
When the appointed hour arrives.  
By then, it’s no longer penance  
Guiding the next step forward,  
But the need to serve—  
In any way possible—  
Offering love’s summons  
To beat back the night.
