Ahh!! I'm drowning  
Deep in the sand.  
It hurts as the grains of hope,  
Cut through my skin.  
It's in my eyes…my hair.  
I yelled …I cried  
For a hand to reach out.  
But I see far ahead-  
the one I followed,  
Lying in a garden and counting the flowers-  
Surrounded by blue birds,  
whose chirps blinded my screams.  
And I lay here alone  
with ravens and crows…  
Screeching and waiting.  
I pulled myself hard…  
But I was weak and tired,  
Tired of my heart-  
The reason behind the pit  
And the sand so warm.  
I was sinking deeper and deeper,  
Not trying anymore  
Letting the sand have its victory  
And letting the birds sing happily beyond me.
