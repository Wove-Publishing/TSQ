This boat, this body

has held

holes, leaks

from side to side,

but what hits

harder than rocks

&nbsp;

is an iceberg,

a world crashing

down for death

to reign on what

deeply sits at

the bottom.

&nbsp;

The cold, sharp chills

have made me

tip over,

while my

boat lost some

&nbsp;

broken parts,

sunken into

the blue sea

where I learn

how to swim

&nbsp;

around the

iceberg,

even climb

over it

to get to

the other

&nbsp;

side, since turns,

steady railings

will lead me on

to safety, to land.
