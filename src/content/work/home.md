My soul is scattered  
across her shores and sands  
inside her abandoned houses  
and her warm valleys  
on sun filled days



I wish to hold her close  
when I catch her scent  
in the air of the afternoon  
light years away



I clutch onto her  
a child refusing to leave his blanket  
like rainwater upon mud  
knowing we will dry up to our ways  
eventually



I exist  
cracked, firm  
in spite of our separation



She is the passing of a life  
A solitary salutation  
in a cold place  
the warmth in my infinite darkness



One day  
I will walk across her endless highways  
in the raindrops of her promised freedom  
touching her colored skies  
that I will once again  
call home
