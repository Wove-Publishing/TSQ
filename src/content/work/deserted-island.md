When I watch 

the water meet 

the shore, 

they touch

each other 

as a connection.



When I watch 

the sun meet 

the peak of 

a mountain, 

they collect light. 



This is isolation 

wanting to wash 

worries, extend

sunshine onto

steep hills.  



This is peace 

wanting to tell

sweet stories 

within storms

and be a village. 



The island waits 

for a soul 

that searches,

stays long 

enough

until waves 

of wonder

emerge.
