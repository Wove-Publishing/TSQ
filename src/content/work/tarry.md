The dead like to tarry.  
Take their sweet time.  
Give the past the hidey ho.  
Contemplate future plans  
Under a banyan tree.  
Luxuriate in the present day.  
And why not; they’ve earned it,  
Tossing off the mortal coil.  
Sounds pretty terrific to me,  
Especially at this moment.  
You know, with the mortgage,  
Each kid’s college education,  
And my wife’s penchant  
For the latest perfume.  
But, hey, I made choices.  
Selected the bed where I sleep.  
Proclaimed my priorities  
To those who listened.  
Now I must ride it out.  
Keep steady at all cost.  
Wait until wisdom arrives,  
When I, too, can linger—  
Dawdle as I please—  
Happy in such serenity,  
With not a care to show for it.
