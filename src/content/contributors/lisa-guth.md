Lisa Guth is a German writer based in Copenhagen, Denmark. She holds an MA in Cognition and Communication from the University of Copenhagen, where she researched representations of feminism in popular television. Her fiction and nonfiction writings explore the intersections of culture, gender, identity and storytelling. Her short story *[Letters from Lucknow, 1886](https://reedsy.com/short-story/akyxkm/)* won a Reedsy writing contest in 2025 and was subsequently featured by [BookTrib](https://booktrib.com/2025/07/25/letters-from-lucknow-1886-lisa-guth/).
