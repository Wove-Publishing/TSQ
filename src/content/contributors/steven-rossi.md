Steven Rossi is a diplomat by day and a poet by night. Based in Maryland, he's been writing for over 8 years and draws his inspiration from his personal experience and Buddhist spiritual practice to examine and appreciate the stillness of daily life. His poems have been previously featured in Tap Into Poetry, Otherwise Engaged, Dazed Santa, Elysian Literary, and Eccentricities Magazine, among others. He’s currently working to get his first collection, *What We Left In February* published. When he's not writing, you can usually find him at a cafe, working out, or hanging out with friends. When the day is done, he enjoys cozying up with his husband -Shane- and their three cats, Panini, Sylvester, and Toby.
