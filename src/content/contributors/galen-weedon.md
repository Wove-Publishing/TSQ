From a young age, Galen has been drawn to stories and to writing. This love was encouraged by his grandfather, who sadly did not live to 

see this accomplishment. Galen is currently editing his debut dark fantasy novel entitled **The Call of the Brimstone Blade**. **This Dead Heart of Mine** is his first published work.

He lives in Melbourne, Australia.
