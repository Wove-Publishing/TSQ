Fidha is a student, poet, and calligraphist who finds comfort in words and the little things people often overlook. She writes about loneliness, rejection, healing, and the feelings that are sometimes too difficult to explain out loud. Her poems are inspired by the complexities of being human, the weight of unspoken thoughts, and the search for meaning in ordinary moments. When she isn't writing, she enjoys exploring different forms of art and finding inspiration in the world around her. Through her poetry, Fidha hopes to make others feel understood, reminding them that even the quietest feelings deserve to be heard.
