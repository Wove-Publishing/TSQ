Jonas V. Muller trained as a natural scientist. Born and raised on the continent, he now lives in Jersey (Channel Islands). He works as a teacher and research consultant. Often, ideas for his short stories come to him in the kitchen while he is preparing supper for his family, or occasionally, while walking along the north coast trail.
