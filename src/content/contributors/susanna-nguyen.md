 Susanna Nguyen is an aspiring writer drawn to works exploring tragedy and complex human behaviour.  She enjoys creative writing and reading fiction. She particularly loves experimenting with different literary forms and styles in her own pieces. Her writing has received recognition in competitions, including the 2026 Write the World People's Choice Award and Just Write Competition, where she was awarded First Place.
